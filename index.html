Retrieval Architecture

The pipeline incorporates query interpretation, guideline routing, clarification prompts, and retrieval from narrative text and structured recommendations.

01

Pre-retrieval interpretation

The incoming query is classified as a guideline question or a case for which clinical detail may be requested.

02

Guideline Router Service

The router selects among the 14 included ESVS guidelines.

03

Context Gate

The system requests missing clinical detail before advising, but this check does not cover all presentations.

04

Dual-channel retrieval

Retrieval uses both narrative guideline text and structured recommendations.

05

Response generation

Responses are generated from published ESVS guidelines and have not been validated for clinical accuracy.

Known limitations.

The system is constrained to 14 ESVS guidelines and will decline questions outside them. It requests missing clinical detail before advising, but this check does not cover all presentations. Its answers have not been assessed for clinical acceptability by expert reviewers; that evaluation is planned. PHI de-identification has not been tested on real data.
